Present value, in plain words

What does "value today" mean?

Money your client pays later is worth less than money today, so every installment is discounted at a fixed yearly rate and added up, then compared with the cash price.

On the unit page

One button on the unit. The answer in front of you.

On a unit card or unit page, tap Calculate NPV and pick a payment plan. You'll see which is better for your client, cash or installments, and by how much.

  1. Open the unit from the project page or your search results.
  2. Pick the plan to compare with the cash discount, if the unit has more than one.
  3. Read the result: which option wins and by how much, with the plan's value today next to the cash price.

If a unit has no cash discount or no complete payment plan, the calculator tells you it can't run the numbers.

Broker questions

Questions about the cash and installment maths

Short answers about the calculator, plan comparison and installment schedule.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp

Is this free?

Yes. The cash-or-installments calculator, plan comparison and installment schedule are all inside Estavo Market, and Estavo Market is completely free for now.

You just need a free sign-up and a verified mobile number to see unit data.

What does "value today" mean?

It's what everything your client pays over the plan is worth in today's money. Each payment is discounted at a fixed 19% a year, and the further away a payment is, the less it's worth today.

If the plan's value today is higher than the cash price after discount, cash is better. If it's lower, installments are better.

Can I change the 19% rate?

No. The app fixes the rate at 19% a year for every unit, so every comparison is made on the same basis.

Is it based on the exact unit price?

It uses the unit type's "from" price, its lowest price, not the price of a specific unit that's available to book.

So confirm the price, plan and cash discount with the developer before you answer your client.

Are installments monthly or quarterly?

Quarterly: one installment every 3 months. There's no monthly schedule at the moment.

The Brokers AI schedule starts from today and leaves out bulk payments and the delivery date, so it can differ from the developer's own schedule.

Is there a rental-yield or investment-return calculator?

No. Return and rental calculations aren't available to brokers yet. The tools here only do payment-plan maths: cash or installments, plan comparison and the installment schedule.

Is the calculator in Arabic?

The plan comparison in Brokers AI shows in Arabic. The NPV calculator on the unit and the chat installment schedule are in English for now.

Can I present this to a client as financial advice?

No. It's maths that helps you explain the difference between cash and installments, not financial advice or an official quote from the developer.
