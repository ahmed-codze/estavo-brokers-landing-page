Frequently asked questions

Questions from brokerage companies

What is the difference between an individual site and a company site?

An individual site centres on the broker's name and their direct relationship with the buyer, A company site is built around the company brand, a shared catalogue and team roles, so several people work on the same foundation

Can the whole sales team use it?

Available roles and the number of users are set at configuration, based on your team size and how you work

Who manages the data and the inventory?

Estavo maintains the selected project data from its own sources, Inventory the company enters remains the company's responsibility to review and update, unless managed data entry has been agreed

Does Client AI appear under the Estavo name?

The assistant appears inside the company site with the site's identity, Name and icon are configured within the settings available at setup

Can we connect the company's Meta account?

Yes, Supported, permitted events reach the company's account, and ownership, audiences and budget remain the company's decision. The permissions required are reviewed when the connection is made

What if we already have a CRM or website?

You can run an Estavo Sites website alongside it, or keep your platform and discuss connecting data and AI as a defined scope from the integration page

What does the company pay for?

Creating the company site is free. The monthly cost depends on the number of team members, the paid cities whose project data appears on the site, and optional services such as a custom domain, managed data entry or a custom integration. What you don’t have to build is the data work: Estavo keeps data on 2,400+ projects from 700 developers updated, so your team doesn’t track prices and payment plans by hand

Does Estavo run our sales or advertising for us?

No. Estavo provides the website, the data and the tools, Campaign decisions, budget, selling and negotiation remain with the company
