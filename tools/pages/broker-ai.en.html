3 examples from the job

From the question to the reply you send your client.

Three situations that come up every day: an incomplete request, a two-project comparison and an offer with clear numbers.

1Is the client's request missing something? It asks you.

Type the request the way the client said it. Estavo lists the conditions it understood, and if something important like handover is unclear, it asks before searching.

Then it shows the matches and close alternatives; anything over budget appears as an alternative.

2Compare two projects in one reply.

Type the two project names. You get price, down payment, installments, areas and amenities side by side, plus one line that sums up the difference.

You can compare up to four projects at once.

What it does, and what it doesn't.

A working assistant for brokers, built on market data. It doesn't replace confirming with the developer, and it isn't an investment adviser.

It does

  • Searches projects for your client's request, and asks about what's missing.
  • Compares two to four projects: prices, plans, areas and amenities.
  • A project's or developer's details, and the projects near a project.
  • Launches published on Estavo, plus brochures, photos and video.
  • A quarterly payment schedule and a PDF offer under your name.
  • In Arabic or English.

It doesn't

  • It doesn't search jobs or read the news brief: those have Jobs and Market news.
  • It gives no investment advice and doesn't calculate returns or ROI.
  • It doesn't know the developer's live availability. Confirm price and availability with them before booking.
  • The PDF offer is a summary from Estavo, not an official quote from the developer.
  • It needs an account: sign up and verify your mobile number.

Questions about Ask Estavo?

Answers to the most common questions about Brokers AI.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp

Is Ask Estavo free?

Yes. Brokers AI is completely free for now inside Estavo Market, with no paid subscription.

You only need to sign up and verify your mobile number.

Where do its answers come from?

From Estavo Market data: projects, developers, payment plans and published launches.

Calculations like the payment schedule use clear assumptions. If the information is not in the data, it tells you.

Are the prices and availability it gives confirmed?

No. It answers from the latest data Estavo received, not the developer's live availability.

Confirm price, availability and booking terms with the developer before you confirm to your client.

Can I talk to it in Arabic?

Yes, in Arabic or English, or both mixed the way you speak.

Some cards, like the payment schedule, have English headings for now.

How is the payment schedule calculated?

From the price, down-payment share and number of years: the down payment today, then equal quarterly installments.

It does not include handover payments or discounts, and it is not necessarily the developer's schedule.

Does the PDF offer carry my name?

Yes, the offer carries your name and number so you can send it to your client.

It summarises the information on Estavo; it is not an official price quote or booking confirmation from the developer.

Can it advise me where to invest or calculate returns?

No. It gives no investment advice and does not calculate rental yield or ROI.

To compare cash with installments at today's value, see the "Cash or installments?" page.

What's the difference from Client AI?

Brokers AI is for you as a broker, inside Estavo Market. Client AI is the assistant for the visitors of your Estavo Sites website.

Client AI answers only from the units shown on your site, and follows your site's package.
