How it works

Search, read, then apply at the source.

We gather open roles every day. You narrow them down to what fits and apply where the job was posted.

  1. Collected every day

    From job websites and official employer careers pages, with closed listings removed.

  2. Filter to fit

    By employer, location, job type, experience level and job website, or only jobs that state a salary. Every option shows how many jobs match.

  3. Read organised details

    About the role, responsibilities and requirements, organised from the original listing where available.

  4. Apply on the original listing

    "Apply" opens the listing on its own website in a new tab, and you finish your application there.

Board rules

Only open roles make the board.

So you don't waste time on a closed ad, every job on the board carries a clear date and source.

  • Closed listings are removed. So are listings past their application deadline.
  • Every listing is dated. The date it was posted on the source, or a plain note when the source doesn't give one.
  • Undated listings must be recently checked. They appear only if checked on their source within the last 7 days.
  • The source is on every job. The job website or employer careers page, where you apply.

Plainly

What the jobs board does, and what it doesn't.

What you get

  • Real-estate jobs in Egypt in one place, refreshed daily.
  • Filters for employer, location, job type, experience, job website and stated salary, with a count beside every option.
  • Organised job details, where the original listing provides them.
  • A direct link to the original listing to apply.
  • An Arabic and English interface.

What you don't

  • No applying inside Estavo. We don't ask for your CV or send it anywhere.
  • No new-job alerts and no saved jobs.
  • Employers don't post on Estavo. Listings come from their sources.
  • Not every real-estate job in Egypt.
  • Details aren't available for every listing, and some listings have no posting date.
  • An employer logo on a listing doesn't mean we verified the employer.

Jobs questions

Questions about the jobs board?

Short answers on where jobs come from, how often they refresh and how you apply.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp

Is the jobs board free?

Yes. It's free for now for any broker registered on Estavo with a verified phone number, with no paid subscription.

Can I apply through Estavo?

No. "Apply" opens the original listing in a new tab, and you complete your application on that website.

Estavo doesn't take applications, doesn't ask for your CV and doesn't send it to anyone.

Where do the jobs come from?

From job websites and official careers pages of real-estate employers. Every listing shows its source.

Job titles and employer details keep the language of the original listing.

How often are jobs updated?

Every day. Listings that close or pass their application deadline are removed from the board.

Even so, check the terms and availability on the source before you apply.

Why do some jobs have no posting date?

Some sources don't publish a posting date. Those listings sit in their own section, and appear only if they were checked on their source within the last 7 days.

"Last checked" is not the date posted.

Does it list every real-estate job in Egypt?

No. We collect from selected sources, so not every real-estate job in Egypt will be here.

I run a company. Can I post a job on Estavo?

No, job posting isn't available. Listings come from job websites and official employer careers pages.
