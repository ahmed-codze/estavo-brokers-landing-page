Privacy and consent

Exactly what is sent, when, and under whose permission.

Before you connect, you should know what data moves and who is responsible for it.

Event categories
Defined, permitted events that describe activity on the site, such as a project viewed or an area selected.
Consent and notice
The connection operates within the notice and consent required on your site and your account settings.
Permissions
You grant permissions on your own Meta assets, and you can review or withdraw them at any time.
Minimum necessary data
Only what the event requires is sent. No chat text or contact numbers appear in event properties.
Retention and deletion
Retention period and deletion route are set at configuration and reviewed alongside the privacy policy.
No inferred intent
No hidden scoring of purchase intent, and no labelling a person as qualified from browsing.

Final privacy and consent wording is reviewed legally before activation.

Before you connect

Questions about the Meta connection.

Whose Meta account is used?

Yours. The Meta Business, Dataset/Pixel and Ad Account stay in your name, and connecting does not transfer ownership to Estavo.

I already have a Pixel and an agency. Can they stay?

Yes. Your agency or media buyer can continue managing campaigns, while Estavo supplies the connection and the approved events.

Do I have to connect Meta for the website to work?

No. The website works without it. The Meta connection is an optional capability that adds context to your account.

What kind of events are sent?

Defined, permitted categories describing site activity, such as a project viewed, an area selected, a property compared or contact submitted. The available categories are confirmed when the connection is made.

Does Estavo manage campaigns and budget?

No. There is no daily optimisation and no automatic budget decisions from Estavo. Those decisions stay with you, your team or your agency.

Does connecting guarantee better leads or sales?

No. The connection provides clearer context, but the outcome also depends on creative, offer, budget, follow-up and market conditions.

Do you see my customers' data?

Events describe site activity with the minimum necessary data. Chat text and contact numbers are not sent in event properties, and permissions on your Meta assets remain under your control.

Can I publish an ad directly from Estavo?

Direct publishing from inside Estavo is confirmed at setup based on what is available in your account. The core connection covers sending permitted events to your account.
