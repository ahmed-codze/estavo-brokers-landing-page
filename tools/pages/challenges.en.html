Fair play

Same rules for everyone, Estavo AI included.

Who judges, what answers are checked against, and how Arabic and English names count.

  1. Estavo AI picks first.

    If it’s in the room, it picks its answer from the verified answers before the round opens, and never sees yours.

  2. Arabic and English are one answer.

    Write the name in Arabic, in English or with a small spelling difference. The same place under two names counts as a repeat.

  3. The host has the final word.

    The AI won’t accept anything outside the verified answers, and anything it’s unsure about goes to the host to decide.

  4. Estavo’s verified data.

    Answers are checked against Estavo’s project data, dated 3 November 2026, for direct sales from developers only, not resale.

Plainly

What’s in the game, and what isn’t.

What it has

  • Questions in Arabic; answer in Arabic or English.
  • 100 fixed questions, mixed automatically and getting harder each round.
  • 2–10 players including Estavo AI; without it, games start at 3 players.
  • 5 rounds, with 30, 45, 60 or 90 seconds per round.
  • Private rooms by link or code, or public rooms listed as available.

What it doesn’t

  • No accounts or profiles, and it isn’t linked to your Estavo account.
  • No public broker leaderboard, prizes or certificates. Standings stay inside the room.
  • Prices in questions are a snapshot dated 3 November 2026, not current offers.
  • No resale or rental questions, and no topic picker.
  • No in-game chat. Talk in person or on a call.

Brokers’ questions

Questions about Exclusive?

Short answers about playing, scoring, judging and the data.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It’s free, and there’s no account. You type a temporary nickname that only the players in your room see, and you start.

The game isn’t linked to your Estavo account, even if you have one.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Keep Estavo AI in the room as a player and practise against it.

Without the AI, a game starts at 3 players, and a room holds up to 10 including the AI.

How is scoring worked out?

A right answer nobody else chose scores 100 points. If two or more players give the same answer, even one in Arabic and one in English, each of them scores zero for it.

Wrong or blank answers score zero.

Who decides if an answer is right?

An AI judge from Estavo checks every answer against the verified answers in Estavo’s data, and understands Arabic and English names and small spelling differences.

Anything it isn’t sure about goes to the host, who has the final say.

Are the questions in English?

No, the questions are in Arabic. But you can write the project, developer or area name in Arabic or in English.

Are the prices in the questions current?

No. Price and unit-type questions use Estavo data dated 3 November 2026, for direct sales from developers only, not resale.

They aren’t current sales offers, so confirm any price with the developer before you quote it to a client.

Are there prizes or a public leaderboard?

No. Standings live inside the room only. There are no prizes, public leaderboards or certificates, and no results are saved under your name.

The game is for practice and friendly competition with your colleagues.

How do I invite colleagues?

Once your room is ready, tap “Share room” and send the link on WhatsApp or copy it, or just send them the room code.

Private rooms are joined by link or code only; public rooms appear in the list of available rooms.
