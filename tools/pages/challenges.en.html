Spy · الجاسوس

You all know the project… except the spy

Each player sees a secret card for an Estavo project, developer or city. The spy only knows which type it is

  • One card each: it opens on your phone only and hides as soon as you leave the page
  • 3 minutes of questions: out loud, in person or on a call, without saying the name

What the games include, and what they don’t

Included

  • Three group games, each with a room, a link and a code
  • Questions and cards from Estavo’s reviewed project, developer and city data
  • Solo practice with Estavo AI in Taghyeer Wesh
  • Arabic and English interface; Taghyeer Wesh questions are in Arabic
  • The host can remove an absent player before starting

Not included

  • No accounts or profiles, and no link to your Estavo account
  • No public broker leaderboard or prizes; rankings stay in the room
  • Prices in questions are a dated snapshot, not current offers
  • No in-game voice chat; talk in person or on a call
  • Spy and Steal the Deal need at least 3 players

Broker questions

Questions about the games?

Short answers about accounts, play, scoring and data

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp

Are the games free? Do I need an account?

Free, with no account. Type a temporary nickname that only the players in your room see, and start.

The games aren’t linked to your Estavo account, even if you have one.

Which game should I start with?

Together or on a call: Spy.

Alone, or to revise names: Taghyeer Wesh with Estavo AI. For competition and bluffing: Steal the Deal.

Do we need to be in the same place?

No. Everyone plays on their own phone through the link.

In Spy you ask questions out loud, so if you’re apart, start a call.

Can I play alone?

In Taghyeer Wesh, yes: keep Estavo AI in the room as a player and practise.

Spy and Steal the Deal need at least 3 players.

Where do the questions and cards come from?

From Estavo’s reviewed project, developer and city data; a few Steal the Deal questions come from official sources.

Prices are a dated snapshot, so confirm any price with the developer before quoting a client.

Are there prizes or a public leaderboard?

No. Rankings stay inside the room, there are no prizes or public leaderboard, and no results are saved under your name.

How do I invite colleagues?

After creating the room, tap “Share room” and send the link on WhatsApp or copy it, or send them the room code.
