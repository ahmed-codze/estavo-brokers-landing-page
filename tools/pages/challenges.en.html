Spy

Everyone knows the project except one player

Players see a card naming a project, developer or city. The spy sees only the category and must work out the answer from your questions

  • Take turns asking a colleague one question without saying the name
  • After 3 minutes, everyone votes on their phone for the player they suspect
  • If the spy is caught, they get one last chance to guess the card from a set of options
  • You win if you catch all the spies and the final guess is wrong. If the vote is tied, the spy escapes
  • The host chooses projects, developers, cities or a mix, and whether the spy sees just the category or a general clue too

Steal the Deal

Write a wrong answer your colleagues will believe, and find the right one

Each round asks about a developer or project. Everyone writes a plausible wrong answer, then looks for the correct answer among all your submissions

  • Answers can be names or numbers, in Arabic or English, up to 6 words
  • For your first game, choose Beginner: 90 seconds to write and 45 to choose
  • Standard mode gives you 60 seconds to write and 30 to choose
  • +3Correct answer
  • +1Each colleague you fool (up to 2)
  • +5Game completed
  • +10First place

3–10 players · About 10 minutes · If you disconnect, return in the same browser to recover your answer and points

Broker questions

Questions about the games?

Accounts, play and points

Are the games free? Do I need an account?

They are free, and you can play with just your name and no account. Sign in for your points to count, even if the game has already started

Which challenge should I start with?

If you are together or on a call, start with Spy. On your own, try Taghyeer Wesh with Estavo AI. For a competitive game, play Steal the Deal

Do we need to be in the same place?

No, everyone joins through the link on their own phone. Questions in Spy are spoken, so get on a call if you are apart

Can I play on my own?

Yes, in Taghyeer Wesh, with Estavo AI or in a public room. Spy and Steal the Deal need at least 3 players

Where do the questions come from?

From the Estavo database of projects, developers and cities, with some Steal the Deal questions from official sources. Prices reflect when they were recorded

How do points and rankings work?

Sign in and play a game with enough signed-in accounts: 3 for Taghyeer Wesh, or 2 with Estavo AI, 3 for Steal the Deal, and 4 for Spy. Games add up to 120 points per day, and your points and rank appear only in your profile

How do I invite colleagues?

After creating a room, share the link on WhatsApp or send the room code. Everyone joins on their own phone
