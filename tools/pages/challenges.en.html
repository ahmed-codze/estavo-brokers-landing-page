Spy

Ask without giving the secret away

Players know the topic. Spies try to work it out from your questions

  • Secret card: an Estavo project, developer or city. Fewer than half the players are spies
  • Take turns: each player asks one question aloud, together or on a call
  • Secret vote: after 3 minutes, or earlier if the host starts it, everyone votes on their phone. The most-voted players are then revealed
  • Last chance: a caught spy guesses the topic from options. Players win if all spies are caught and the guess is wrong. A tied vote lets the spy escape
  • Host choices: mixed, developers, cities or projects. Spy help shows the category alone or adds a general fact

Broker questions

Questions about the games?

Accounts, play and points

Are the games free? Do I need an account?

They are free and you can play with a nickname without an account. sign in to add counted-match points to Estavo, even mid-match

Which game should I try first?

Together or on a call: Spy. solo or to practise names: Taghyeer Wesh with Estavo AI. for competitive bluffing: Steal the Deal

Do we need to be in the same place?

No, each player uses their own phone with the link or code. Spy questions are spoken, so use a call if you are apart

Can I play solo?

Yes, Taghyeer Wesh has solo practice with Estavo AI and public rooms. group play supports 3–10 players. Spy and Steal the Deal need at least 3 players

Where do questions and cards come from?

From Estavo’s catalogue of projects, developers and cities. prices and availability are dated snapshots, and a unit type appearing in a game does not confirm it is for sale today

How do points and ranking work?

Sign in and play a counted match: Taghyeer Wesh needs 3 accounts, or 2 with Estavo AI. Steal the Deal 3. Spy 4. below that, it is a friendly match with no Estavo points added

Games add up to 120 Estavo points daily, with 3 counted matches per day for the same group. your profile shows monthly and total points, your ranks and the gap to the next rank. ranking counts broker accounts with verified phone numbers. there is no public leaderboard page

How do I invite colleagues?

Create a room and share its link on WhatsApp, copy it, or send the room code. everyone opens it on their own phone
