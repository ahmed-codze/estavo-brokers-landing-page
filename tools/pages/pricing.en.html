Website visitors and enquiries submitted through the site are unlimited. The allowance applies only when you or your team deliberately creates a customer record inside Estavo to track calls, walk-ins, referrals or other opportunities. When it runs out, add a lead pack without buying another city.

Pricing questions.

Is Estavo Market free?

Yes. The broker-facing Market experience is free; optional AI and PDF tools use credits.

Is an individual website free?

Yes. Website creation starts free in around five minutes. A newly created site has zero assigned cities and zero tracked-lead allowance until Estavo applies a launch grant or paid package.

Do website enquiries use my monthly lead allowance?

No. Visitors and inbound website enquiries are unlimited. Only customer records deliberately created by your team for tracking consume the monthly allowance.

Must I buy another city when I need more leads?

No. Choose a monthly lead top-up for your existing package. The exact approved prices are shown above and in your plan screen.
