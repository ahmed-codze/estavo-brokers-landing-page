Individual broker

City-count pricing with clear monthly allowances.

The monthly paid-city schedule is EGP 450 for one city, EGP 680 for two, EGP 820 for three, then EGP 115 for each additional city. Each paid city includes 50 new Client Tracking Links and 100 Website AI messages per month.

50 Client Tracking Links × paid cities

100 Website AI messages × paid cities. The separate launch grant remains one free city with five monthly Tracking Links. Existing tracked clients remain available; only newly created links consume the allowance.

Optional usage

Link packs: +25 for EGP 150 · +50 for EGP 250 · +100 for EGP 400 · +250 for EGP 800 per month. A custom AI allowance can be agreed independently.

Website visitors and enquiries submitted through the website remain unlimited.

Brokerage company

The company package starts at three members and expands progressively.

First choose the number of paid cities using the same city-count rate as an individual. Then apply the member discount progressively: members 1–3 pay 90%, 4–10 pay 85%, 11–20 pay 80%, and members 21+ pay 75%. Crossing a band changes only the new members in that band.

Included monthly usage

Client Tracking Links = members × paid cities × 50.

Website AI messages = members × paid cities × 100.

Both allowances are pooled across the company website. Member discounts reduce price, not allowances.

Company usage top-ups

Link packs: +75 for EGP 450 · +150 for EGP 750 · +300 for EGP 1,200 · +750 for EGP 2,400 per month. A custom AI allowance can be agreed independently.

Repeated visits through an existing client link do not consume another link.

Example: 7 members · 3 cities · +300 links

Three members at 90% plus four members at 85% makes the base EGP 5,002/month. The package includes 1,050 links and 2,100 AI messages; the +300 pack raises links to 1,350 and the monthly total to EGP 6,202.

Billing periods

Discount the base, not the usage add-ons.

Monthly has no billing discount. Quarterly is base × 3 × 90%. Annual is base × 12 × 60%. Client Tracking Link packs remain at their full recurring price for every month in the term.

Separate charges

Custom domains, future AI top-ups and taxes are outside the package unless they are expressly written into the signed offer. Existing active company subscriptions stay on their recorded pricing version until they are deliberately changed.

Pricing questions.

Does a company get a free first city?

No. Every city selected for the new company package is paid and contributes to the package calculation and both usage allowances.

What exactly uses a Client Tracking Link?

Creating a new tracked client link uses one allowance unit. Repeat visits on that same link do not. Historical tracked clients remain accessible after the monthly reset.

Does adding a city increase AI messages?

Yes. Both paid cities and team members increase the pooled Website AI allowance: 100 messages per member per paid city each month.

Can Sales prepare a custom deal?

Yes. The approved calculator supplies the defaults, and the final signed offer can record a custom amount and negotiated tracking-link or AI allowance.
