Frequently asked questions

Everything to know before you open Estavo Market.

Straight answers about the product, its tools and its limits.

Is Estavo Market free?

Yes. Searching, browsing and comparing in the broker-facing Market are free. Optional Brokers AI and branded PDF tools use credits.

Is it a CRM or a consumer property portal?

No. It is the market from the broker’s side: inventory is organised for you to search, compare and sell, not for the end client to browse. It does not replace pipeline management.

Does Brokers AI replace the broker?

No. It helps you search, compare and prepare the response; you review the information before sending it to the client.

What uses credits?

Brokers AI messages and PDF-offer generation. Searching, browsing and comparing in the Market are free.

What are the boundaries of the market data and PDF offer?

Estavo follows price and payment-plan updates. Any fast-changing item or detail that needs developer confirmation is shown clearly in the offer; the PDF is an Estavo summary, not a developer contract or official quotation.
