Data source

Data you can trace to its source.

Estavo collects project information from its sources, reviews it and places it into fixed fields. Every update goes through the same cycle.

  1. Arrives

    From developers: price lists, brochures and update messages.

  2. Reviewed

    The Estavo team reviews and structures it into fixed fields, rather than copying it as-is.

  3. Published

    On the project and unit in Estavo Market, ready to search and compare.

  4. Updated

    Any change to a price or payment plan goes through the same review.

To be clear: price, availability and booking terms can change. Confirm them with the developer before contracting.

Before you open the market.

Straight answers about the product and its limits.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp

Is Estavo Market free?

Yes, completely free for now: search, comparison, Brokers AI and PDF offers. If a paid service is ever added, it will be clear before you use it.

Who is it for?

Anyone working on real-estate client requests: freelance brokers, sales teams at brokerages, and team leads who need their team to answer faster and more accurately.

Does it have every project and unit in Egypt?

No. It has the projects and unit types Estavo follows in the cities it works in, and that keeps growing. If a project isn't there, it won't be shown as if it were.

Are prices and availability live?

No. Estavo follows price and payment-plan updates and reviews them before publishing, but price, availability and booking terms need final confirmation from the developer.

How is Brokers AI different from ChatGPT?

It answers from the project and unit data in Estavo Market, not a general web search. It asks when a request is incomplete and tells you when information isn't available. The decision stays yours.

Is it a CRM or a listings site?

No. It's the broker's side of the market: search, compare and prepare your reply. It isn't a site for end buyers, and it doesn't replace pipeline management.

Is the PDF an official developer offer?

No. It's a clean Estavo summary with your name and contact details, for review with the client. It isn't a contract or an official developer quotation.
