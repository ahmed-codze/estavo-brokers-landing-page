How we prepare the market data

We collect project data, review it and keep it updated.

Estavo collects project data from developer price lists, brochures and updates, and lays it out in one consistent format so you can search and compare easily.

  1. We collect it

    From developer price lists, brochures and the updates we receive.

  2. We review it

    The Estavo team reviews the information and organises it into clear fields for price, down payment, payment plan, handover and other project details.

  3. We publish it

    After review, the data appears in Estavo Market, ready to search and compare.

  4. We update it

    When an update arrives for a price, payment plan or project detail, it goes through the same review before publishing.

Important: prices, unit availability and payment plans can change at the developer. Before you confirm a price or availability to your client, check the latest update with the developer.

Is Estavo Market free?

Yes. Search, comparison, Brokers AI and PDF offers are free for now, with no paid subscription.

If paid services are ever added, their details and price will be clear before you use them.

Who is Estavo Market for?

Freelance brokers, sales teams at real-estate marketing companies, and anyone whose work is following projects and developers and answering client requests.

Whether you work on one project or compare options across developers, Estavo Market helps you find the information you need.

Does it have every project and unit in Egypt?

No, not every project or every unit in Egypt.

Estavo has data for 2,400+ projects and 26,000+ unit types across 16 cities, as of the latest published count. That number describes the size of the database, not the number of units available to book right now.

Are prices and availability updated live?

No, not live.

We update the data when new information reaches us and has been reviewed. Prices, availability and booking terms can change at the developer before an update reaches us, so confirm the final price and availability with the developer before booking.

How is Brokers AI different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general assistant. Brokers AI is built to help brokers with real-estate work, and uses Estavo Market data to search and compare projects and payment plans.

You can write the client's request your way; it helps you review the options available in the data and tells you when information is missing.

Is Estavo Market a CRM or a listings site?

No. Estavo Market is a tool to search project data, compare options and prepare replies and offers for clients.

If you need a property website under your name or your company's, that is a separate Estavo product called Estavo Sites.

Is the PDF an official developer offer?

No. The PDF is a tidy summary of the information on Estavo, carrying your name and number so you can send it to your client.

It does not replace an official developer quotation or booking confirmation.
