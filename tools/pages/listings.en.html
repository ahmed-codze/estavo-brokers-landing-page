Before you list

Questions about listing and distribution.

Is this for resale only, or primary too?

Presenting on your website covers your own properties and the market projects you choose to show. Network distribution starts with eligible resale properties, and the final scope is confirmed at setup.

Does the property have to appear on my site first?

The website is the foundation. The property is added and presented under your name, and you can then enable distribution for eligible properties.

Who sets the commission?

You do. The rate brokers see in the network is the one you enter in the property details.

Does Estavo take the deal or make contact instead of me?

No. Estavo provides the presentation and distribution layer. Contact, negotiation and the deal remain with you as the listing broker.

Does reaching the network guarantee a sale?

No. Distribution widens the chance of reaching the right broker, but it does not guarantee any particular reach or a sale.

Who is responsible for updating price and status?

You are responsible for the properties you enter yourself, unless a managed data-entry service has been agreed.

Can I remove the property once it is sold?

Yes. You can change the status, stop the listing or remove the property from your site and from the network.

Is the data-entry service paid?

Entering your own properties is part of the website. Managed data entry is a separate scope agreed at setup.
