What is Estavo Sites?

Your website starts full.

A fair question

“I already use Instagram and WhatsApp. Why do I need a website?”

Instagram creates reach. Your website closes the loop.

A post disappears down the feed; your website remains. Stories are not searchable; your website lets buyers search for themselves. And WhatsApp works better after the buyer has already seen the details.

The website does not replace those channels—it is where they lead.

Before you begin

Questions about your site.

Is the website itself free?

Website creation, identity and core pages are free. Optional additions show their terms before you choose them.

Who runs the hosting and server?

Estavo operates the site infrastructure under the current service terms. Any custom domain or different operating request is defined during setup.

Does the site carry Estavo's name or mine?

The customer-facing site carries the name, identity and contact details you select. Estavo operates the service behind it.

Can I use my own domain?

Domain options, ownership and renewal terms are explained during setup before confirmation.

Can I add primary and resale properties myself?

You can add the property types supported by your service. You remain responsible for the accuracy and updates of properties you enter.

Is the site bilingual and mobile-friendly?

Available language and page options are shown during setup, and every published interface is designed to remain usable on mobile.
