To be clear

What you'll find, and what you won't.

A developer page brings their data together in one place. A few things are worth knowing before you rely on it.

You'll find

  • Each developer's overview, founded year, website and accounts.
  • Their projects on Estavo, filtered by city, area and type.
  • Their Estavo Daily news, next to their data.
  • Sales-team numbers and the company profile, where available.
  • Brokers AI answering about the developer page you have open.

You won't find

  • Live data: we update when new information reaches us and has been reviewed.
  • Sales numbers or a company profile for every developer.
  • Ratings, rankings or scores of developers.
  • Access without an account: sign up and verify your mobile number.
  • Price or booking confirmation: that happens with the developer.

Broker questions

Questions about developer pages?

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp

Are developer pages free?

Yes. Sign up free, verify your mobile number, and you can open the developer directory, developer pages and their projects.

Estavo Market is completely free for now, with no cut of your commission.

Does it include every developer in Egypt?

No. It has data on 700 developers as of 3 October 2026, alongside data on 2,400+ projects.

We add developers and projects as we collect and review their data.

Are sales-team numbers there for every developer?

No. They appear on a developer's page only when Estavo has them.

When they're missing, you can reach the developer's website and accounts from the same page.

Is the data updated live?

No, not live. We update the data when new information reaches us and has been reviewed.

Prices and availability can change at the developer before an update reaches us, so confirm them with the developer before replying to a client.

Does Estavo rate or rank developers?

No. There are no ratings, rankings or trust scores.

The page shows the developer's data, projects and news. The judgement is yours and your client's.

What does Brokers AI know about a developer?

It knows which developer page you have open and answers from Estavo's data: founded year, head office and website, plus their projects, cities and price ranges.

You can also ask which developers have units matching your client's request.

Where does developer news come from?

From the market news Estavo Daily collects. Every story linked to the developer shows on their page, with its sources.
