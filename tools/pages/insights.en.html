How the brief is made

We sum up the story in our words. The source is always one tap away

Estavo doesn’t copy articles. We write a short summary and link you to the original story from the outlet that published it

  1. We follow the sources

    News and business outlets that write about property and the market

  2. We group each story

    A story published in several places appears once, with all its sources

  3. We write our own summary

    A headline and two lines, without copying the article

  4. We link and rank it

    To the city, developer and project when it’s clear, most important first

And launches? Before one is published, we match it to its project in our data, and the Estavo team reviews any that aren’t clear
