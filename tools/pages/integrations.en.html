Delivery and updates

Two ways the data reaches your system.

We choose the method with your technical team. Either way, the data passes review by Estavo's team before it reaches you.

Your system pulls

A secured Estavo API

  • An account and key of your own, handed over through a secure link that opens once.
  • Pull the full catalogue once, then only what changed since your last pull — removals included.
  • Search unit types with filters, or by down payment and monthly instalment.

Estavo pushes · by arrangement

Updates pushed to your server

  • Each project in full — developer, unit types and payment plans — to an HTTPS endpoint you provide.
  • Projects, schedule and the mapping between your records and Estavo's are agreed before the first send.
  • Every batch has a fixed ID and an acknowledgement, so a resend never duplicates data.

And AI? A separate branch, if you want it.

You don't need AI to take the data. If you want an assistant answering buyers inside your platform, there's a separate chat interface that searches the listings you send to Estavo — and, if agreed, developer inventory when your listings have no match or the buyer asks for it.

The assistant's name, tone and Arabic or English welcome message are set for your account, and owner details never appear in its recommendations.

Integration questions

Before we get into details.

Short answers to what companies and platforms ask us most.

Question not here? Message us on WhatsApp

Do I need a new website?

No. The integration brings the data into the website, app or system you already have, shown in your platform's design and brand.

Can I take the data only, without AI?

Yes. Data on its own is a complete offer. The AI assistant is a separate branch and is not required to get the data.

How are cost and timing decided?

By the cities, data scope, integration method, usage rights and support. There is no fixed published price; both are set out in the proposal after we review your needs. AI cost depends on usage.

How fresh is the data?

Not real-time. We update the data when new information reaches us and has been reviewed, and your system receives the changes on an agreed schedule. Final price and availability must be confirmed with the developer before booking.

Is there public documentation or a self-service key?

No. Once we agree, we set up an account and key for you, delivered through a secure link that opens once, with a Postman collection for your account. The key belongs on your server, not in page code your visitors can see.

Does it include every project in Egypt?

No. As of 3 October 2026, Estavo has data on 700 developers, 2,400+ projects, 26,000+ unit types, and 110 areas in 16 cities. That is the size of the database, not the number of units available to book.
