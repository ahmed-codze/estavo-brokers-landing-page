Before you enable it

Questions about Client AI.

Is it just ChatGPT on my website?

No. It is connected to the property information on your site, so it searches and compares inside your inventory instead of answering in general terms.

Does the AI know all of Estavo Market?

No. It stays within the projects and units available to your site.

Can it see another company's data?

No. Each site has its own scope, and the assistant does not reach another customer's data.

Does the buyer have to leave a number first?

No. They can ask and compare first, and share contact details when they decide to continue.

Can it compare and calculate instalments?

It can explain and compare the fields available to it. Final price, availability and booking still need confirmation from the source or from you.

What if there is no match or the information is missing?

It states that the information is unavailable, asks a useful question where relevant, offers a clearly labelled in-scope alternative, and makes it easy to reach you.

Does it appear under the Estavo name?

The assistant appears inside your website with your site's identity. Name and icon are configured within the settings available at setup.

Does it replace the sales team?

No. It helps at the start of the search and hands the conversation to your team when the buyer wants to continue.
