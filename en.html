Questions before you start.

What exactly does Estavo do?

It brings together market data, the properties you sell and your clients' interests, then uses AI to analyse how they relate — so you can see which property fits which client.

Is Estavo a CRM?

Not a traditional one. A CRM is built to manage leads and a sales pipeline. Estavo connects the market, the property and the client, and analyses the relationship between them to help you recommend, compare and market.

What's free?

Searching and comparing in Estavo Market, and your basic real-estate website. City data on your website, AI usage and additional services depend on your plan or usage credit.

Do I need a website to use Estavo?

No. Estavo Market works on its own for search and comparison. The website is for when you want clients to see the same data and AI under your name.

Where do the AI's answers come from?

From the project and unit data available to Estavo. On your website, from the properties and information available in your own experience. When something isn't available, it says so and points the client to you.

Does Estavo know everything in the market?

Estavo collects, organises and updates the data it follows. What's available varies by city, project and type of information, and final unit availability and pricing should be confirmed with the developer before booking.

Where does client information come from?

From what clients choose to share and the interactions recorded in your experience on Estavo. Not every client leaves the same amount of information. Privacy policy
