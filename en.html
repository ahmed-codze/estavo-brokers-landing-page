Before you start

Who is Estavo for?

Real-estate brokers and brokerage companies in Egypt who need market data and news in their daily work, and want a website and marketing under their own name. Platforms can also connect Estavo data to their systems.

Do I need a website?

No. You can use Estavo Market, market news and jobs without a website. A website is an option if you want to present your work and market under your name.

What’s free and what’s paid?

Estavo Market is completely free for now, including Brokers AI and PDF offers. Creating the basic website is free; cities, AI and marketing features on the website follow your plan.

How is the data updated?

Estavo collects developers’ price lists, brochures and updates, reviews them and updates the data when new information arrives. It is not live, so confirm price and availability with the developer before booking.

What does the AI use?

Brokers AI searches the project, developer and launch data in Estavo; for launch questions it may add web information, clearly marked as external, and tells you when information is missing. On your website, Client AI answers only from the properties you show. Privacy policy

Are jobs and games and challenges free? Do I need an account?

Both are free. Jobs are inside your Estavo account, so they need a free sign-up and a verified phone number. Games and challenges are open without an account — just a nickname.
