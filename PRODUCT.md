# Estavo Brokers

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Egyptian independent real estate brokers and brokerage teams. The owner requests public answers to their inventory, buyer matching, sales, client acquisition, and proposal questions, in Arabic and English.

## Product Purpose

Make Estavo discoverable through useful public information and help brokers evaluate the product. Success means accurate discovery and qualified trial visits, not guaranteed AI recommendations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Estavo Market provides project/unit information, search, comparisons, a broker AI assistant and branded PDF offers. Availability and final terms require confirmation before booking. Product truth follows the reviewed September 23 support knowledge package in the adjacent workspace. Estavo Sites is a distinct service. No standalone ROI calculator or automatic sales guarantees are claimed.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing Estavo Brokers name, logos, bilingual voice and incumbent visual identity. This task extends the site with a readable knowledge section.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner approved product/research case studies rather than customer success stories. September 30 research has auditable aggregate counts and public-source pointers. It does not establish realized sales prices, returns or predictive accuracy. Publish original aggregate analysis only; preserve private originals and contact information outside public assets.

## Product Principles

- Answer the broker's question fully before introducing the relevant product workflow.
- Separate verified observations, illustrative examples and recommendations.
- Cite public sources and state the period and limitations of research.
- Keep knowledge usable without JavaScript or a login.
