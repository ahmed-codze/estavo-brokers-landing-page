# Landing Page Brief — Estavo Market

## Route and conversion

- Arabic route: `/market/`
- English route: `/market/en.html`
- Product: Estavo Market
- Primary conversion: `https://brokers.estavo.space/go/?ref=default-landing-page-market`
- Primary event: `market_opened`
- Primary promise: start using the broker market free now

Every primary button on this page goes directly to the Market conversion URL. No primary CTA routes to Pricing, WhatsApp, a contact form or the Estavo Sites builder.

## Why this page exists

This is the one complete commercial page for the broker-facing product. It absorbs all stories about market data, data collection and updates, broker search, comparison, Brokers AI and PDF offers.

It exists because the visitor should not have to understand Estavo's internal service structure before trying a free product. A broker has one question: “Can this help me work my client's request now?”

## Audience

- Freelance broker who searches across WhatsApp, PDFs and developer messages.
- Junior salesperson learning projects and payment plans.
- Senior salesperson who wants faster lookup and comparison.
- Brokerage team that wants routine market questions answered from one organized marketplace.

## Core proposition

Arabic:

> سوق العقارات للبروكر في مكان واحد.

English:

> The property marketplace for brokers, in one place.

Supporting idea: Estavo Market is the broker side of the property market. It brings searchable, comparable inventory together so the broker does not have to open another broker website or wait for another developer WhatsApp reply.

### Hero art

One wide working surface shows:

1. a natural client request;
2. visible filters for area, budget, unit type and payment preference;
3. two matching project/unit rows;
4. a comparison tray;
5. small, subordinate output paths to Brokers AI and a branded PDF.

The Market inventory and comparison surface is the visual anchor. AI and PDF are capabilities inside it, not equal product cards.

Mobile reduces the scene to request → two matches → one selected output. It must not shrink the desktop table.

## Page sections

### 1. كل بيانات السوق اللي تحتاجها في شغلك

Purpose: establish what the broker can find.

Content:

- cities and areas;
- developers and projects;
- unit types and available unit information;
- prices and payment plans where present;
- delivery and project details where present.

English heading: `The market information you need for the request in front of you.`

CTA: `ابدأ البحث مجانًا / Start searching free` → Market URL.

Visual: one recognizable Market search/results screen. Do not use inventory-count claims.

### 2. بدل ما تدور وسط ملفات ورسائل كتير

Purpose: name the existing friction without attacking the sources brokers rely on.

Content: WhatsApp, PDFs and developer messages remain useful inputs, but Estavo makes the relevant information searchable and comparable when a client asks.

English heading: `Use the information instead of hunting for it.`

Visual: scattered source formats converge into one searchable marketplace. Solid connections, no floating file-card menu.

### 3. إستاڤو بيجمع وينظّم ويتابع التحديث

Purpose: explain why the product is more useful than a folder of files.

Workflow:

1. information arrives from approved sources;
2. it is reviewed and structured;
3. it is published into the relevant project/unit fields;
4. later changes enter the same review process.

English heading: `Collected, structured and maintained for use.`

Boundary: do not say all data is live, instant, official or complete. Fast-changing prices and availability need final confirmation.

CTA: `شوف السوق بنفسك / See the market for yourself` → Market URL.

### 4. ابدأ من طلب العميل

Purpose: demonstrate the product's main job.

Use an explicitly labelled example:

> شقة ٣ غرف في القاهرة الجديدة، بميزانية محددة وتقسيط طويل.

Show criteria extraction, exact matches and clearly labelled alternatives. Never silently raise the budget or invent a preference.

English heading: `Start from the client's request.`

### 5. قارن اللي هيحدد الاختيار

Purpose: show decision support, not generic browsing.

Compare only available facts: location, unit type, payment plan, delivery and relevant project details. Explain trade-offs instead of naming an absolute winner.

English heading: `Compare what will decide the choice.`

CTA: `قارن اختيارات عميلك مجانًا / Compare your client's options free` → Market URL.

### 6. Brokers AI يساعدك تشتغل على الطلب

Purpose: place Brokers AI correctly inside Market.

Show that the broker can:

- enter the request naturally in Arabic, English or mixed language;
- ask about a project or payment plan;
- request a comparison or simple explanation;
- ask for a concise draft reply;
- continue with follow-up questions.

Boundaries:

- asks for missing criteria instead of guessing;
- uses Estavo's available data rather than random web search;
- says when information is unavailable;
- does not replace broker judgment or final confirmation.

English heading: `Ask the market in the way you work.`

CTA: `جرّب Brokers AI / Try Brokers AI` → Market URL.

### 7. ابعت عرض باسمك ورقمك

Purpose: explain the branded PDF outcome.

Show a selected project/unit becoming a clean PDF offer with the broker's name and contact details.

Boundary: it is a client-ready Estavo output, not a developer contract or official quotation.

English heading: `Send a client-ready offer carrying your details.`

### 8. المجاني والـ Credits

Purpose: make the business model understandable without weakening “free Market.”

Structure:

- free: ordinary Market browsing/search/comparison as confirmed;
- credits: Brokers AI messages and PDF offers as confirmed;
- purchased credits: state expiry/reset behavior only after finance approval.

Do not make visitors calculate credits in the hero. Keep exact values in one maintainable commercial component.

3. `هل عليه كل مشروع ووحدة في مصر؟`

7. `الـ PDF Offer عرض رسمي من المطور؟`
8. `إيه اللي بيستهلك Credits؟`

- English title direction: `Estavo Market for brokers — search and compare free`
- Description should name projects, units, payment plans, comparisons, Brokers AI and free access without claiming complete/live inventory.

## Claims requiring approval

- exact free-access scope;
- 40 weekly free credits;
- 2 credits per AI message;
- 3 credits per PDF offer;
- contracts/relationships with developers;
- named city/project coverage;
- any update-frequency statement.

## Acceptance criteria

- All data/update/Brokers AI stories live on this page or point here.
- Every primary CTA goes to the Market URL.
- “Free” is visible in the hero and supported by an accurate scope explanation.
- The page does not look like a consumer listing portal.
- Brokers AI is not confused with Client AI.
- Desktop/mobile and animated/still art tell the same request-to-result story.
