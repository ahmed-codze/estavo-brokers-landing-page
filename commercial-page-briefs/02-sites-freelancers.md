# Landing Page Brief — Estavo Sites for Freelance Brokers

## Route and conversion

- Arabic route: `/websites/`
- English route: `/websites/en.html`
- Product: Estavo Sites
- Primary conversion: `https://estavo-brokers.com/website/`
- Primary event: `website_preview_started`

Every primary button begins the Estavo Site creation flow. The page may explain AI, Meta and network distribution, but it must not send the main conversion to those explainer pages.

## Why this page exists

Freelance brokers often build trust personally but send clients to developer pages, portals, PDFs, Instagram or WhatsApp threads where the broker's own identity and selected work are fragmented. This page sells a single professional property destination under the broker's name.

## Audience

- Independent/freelance property broker.
- Salesperson building a personal book of business where company policy permits.
- Broker working through Instagram and WhatsApp without a dedicated website.
- Broker with primary and/or resale inventory to present.

## Core proposition

Owner-approved positioning, 2026-09-20: Estavo Sites gives an independent broker
a customer-facing property website that starts with useful market content instead
of an empty shell. The broker selects Estavo projects and cities, adds personally
managed primary/resale listings and publishes them under their own identity and
contact routes. Initial website creation is free and ready in around five minutes;
optional data, AI, domains and service work keep their own scope.

A broker identity setup—name, logo/color and contact—becomes one live property website. The site shows a broad search/home banner and a small selection of projects/own properties. A buyer chooses WhatsApp/contact with the broker's details still visible.

Browser framing is meaningful here. Do not use a generic SaaS dashboard as the hero.

## Page sections

1. `موقعك بيتولد وهو مليان` explains the product from first principles and
   separates Estavo data, broker-managed listings and broker identity.
2. The Nour Properties example shows populated project and resale cards. It must
   remain labelled as an example until a verified public live-site URL is supplied.
3. `ليه أصلًا موقع؟` explains identity, one link and direct contact. This is the
   second and final place where the technical/data-team comparison appears.
4. The Instagram/WhatsApp objection is visible content, not a closed FAQ item.
5. A three-step sequence covers identity, presented inventory and sharing the link.
6. Pricing separates the free foundation, optional additions and separate scope.
7. One consolidated line explains Estavo-source updates versus broker-entered data.
8. The remaining FAQ handles operational questions without repeating the social objection.
9. A compact bridge connects Sites with Estavo Market under the same account/data.
10. The close offers the free creation flow and WhatsApp as the secondary path.

## Visual system

- Main composition: personal identity → public website → buyer contact.
- Use a light/paper hero with one navy website panel.
- Small property thumbnails may appear inside the site, but the website/product structure remains dominant.
- Mobile: identity header, search, two property rows and WhatsApp/contact in one compact browser surface.

## SEO

- Arabic title direction: `موقع عقاري مجاني للبروكر باسمك — Estavo Sites`
- English title direction: `Free branded property website for brokers — Estavo Sites`
- Description should mention broker identity, selected projects/units, own listings and direct contact.

## Claims requiring approval

- “free indefinitely” and fair-use boundaries;
- included hosting/server support;
- first free city;
- custom-domain pricing/ownership;
- data-entry prices;
- setup steps or optional services beyond the approved “website ready in around five minutes” onboarding proposition. Do not generalize that timing to data, AI, Meta or integration setup.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page speaks to an individual broker, not a company procurement team.
- The website is presented as more than visual design.
- Estavo-managed and broker-managed inventory are visibly distinct.
- Every primary CTA goes to `/website/`.
- AI, Meta and network distribution appear as Sites capabilities, not separate products.
