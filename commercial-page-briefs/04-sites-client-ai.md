# Landing Page Brief — Client AI for Estavo Sites

## Route and conversion

- Arabic route: `/ai/`
- English route: `/ai/en.html`
- Product: Estavo Sites
- Capability: Client AI
- Primary conversion: `https://estavo-brokers.com/website/`
- Primary event: `website_preview_started`
- Demo event where a prepared example exists: `ai_demo_completed`

This page is not the home of Brokers AI. Brokers AI belongs inside the Estavo Market page. The page's primary action is to create/configure an Estavo Site.

## Why this page exists

A normal property site can show information but still leaves buyers to search, interpret payment details and decide what to ask. Client AI turns the broker's own site into a guided conversation while staying inside the inventory that broker or company chose to present.

## Audience

- Freelance broker considering an Estavo Site.
- Brokerage owner or marketing lead.
- Sales team that repeats the same project/payment answers.
- Company that wants a buyer-facing assistant under its own identity.

## Core proposition

Arabic:

> AI يساعد عميلك يدور ويسأل ويقارن على موقعك إنت.

English:

> AI that helps buyers search, ask and compare on your own website.

### Hero art

Show Client AI inside a brokerage website—not as an independent chatbot floating beside it.

Example sequence:

1. buyer asks for a three-bedroom apartment in a named area;
2. assistant asks for one missing criterion such as budget/payment preference;
3. assistant returns two matching properties from the site's allowed inventory;
4. buyer can open the property or continue to WhatsApp.

The assistant uses a customer-configurable identity if that capability is approved. Do not use the Estavo mark as the customer-facing avatar.

## Page sections

### 1. مش Live Chat عادي

Explain that Client AI can use structured property information to search, clarify and compare. A traditional live chat is only a conversation channel.

English: `More than a chat box.`

### 2. عارف الداتا اللي على موقعك—وبس

Show the scope boundary:

- selected Estavo project/city data on the Site;
- broker/company primary and resale inventory made available to the assistant;
- no access to another company's data;
- no automatic access to all Estavo Market inventory.

English: `It knows the inventory on your site—and stays inside that scope.`

### 3. يبدأ من سؤال العميل الطبيعي

Supported request ingredients:

- area;
- budget;
- property/unit type;
- payment preference;
- named project;
- follow-up comparison.

Show Arabic, English and mixed-language examples.

English: `Buyers can ask in the way they naturally speak.`

### 4. لو الطلب ناقص، يسأل. مش يفترض.

Demonstrate clarification and state changes in the same conversation. If the buyer changes budget or area, the latest explicit request controls the next answer.

English: `When the request is incomplete, it asks instead of guessing.`

### 5. يشرح ويقارن من الداتا المتاحة

Capabilities where data exists:

- project/unit explanation;
- prices and payment plans;
- installment calculations;
- comparisons and trade-offs;
- clearly labelled alternatives when no exact match exists.

Final prices, availability and booking terms require human/source confirmation.

English: `Explain and compare what is actually available to the assistant.`

### 6. يساعد الأول، من غير ما يزن على العميل

The buyer can begin without immediately giving a name or phone number. Do not turn every conversation into a forced form.

English: `Help first. Ask for contact when the buyer is ready.`

### 7. من المحادثة لفريق الـ Sales

Show a clean handoff to WhatsApp or the team. The company can use the buyer's explicitly shared request and conversation context subject to approved notice, consent, access and retention rules.

Do not claim secret intent scoring or guaranteed lead quality.

English: `Hand the conversation to sales with useful context.`

### 8. جزء من براندك

Subject to product validation, explain configurable:

- assistant name;
- Arabic/English welcome message;
- tone;
- icon/avatar.

Do not promise arbitrary personality training or unlimited customization.

English: `A buyer experience that belongs inside your brand.`

### 9. لما المعلومة مش موجودة

Approved behavior:

1. state that the information is unavailable;
2. ask a useful clarification where relevant;
3. offer an in-scope alternative only when clearly labelled;
4. make human contact easy.

English: `A useful “I don't know” is better than an invented answer.`

### 10. FAQ

Recommended questions:

1. `هل هو مجرد ChatGPT على الموقع؟`
2. `الـ AI يعرف كل Estavo Market؟`
3. `يقدر يشوف داتا شركة تانية؟`
4. `هل لازم العميل يسيب رقمه الأول؟`
5. `يقدر يقارن ويحسِب الأقساط؟`
6. `لو مفيش Match أو المعلومة ناقصة يعمل إيه؟`
7. `هل يظهر باسم Estavo؟`
8. `هل بيستبدل فريق الـ Sales؟`

## Visual system

- Dark navy conversation area contained inside a white/paper brokerage website.
- Distinguish buyer and assistant messages through shape and alignment, not bright colors.
- Matching results should look like real property rows, not promotional cards.
- Motion: restrained typing dots and one response reveal; no endlessly moving chat bubbles.
- Reduced motion: show the complete final answer and static dots.
- Mobile: one conversation and two compact results inside one 390px composition.

## SEO

- Arabic title direction: `AI عقاري لعملاء موقعك — Estavo Sites`
- English title direction: `Property AI for buyers on your website — Estavo Sites`
- Description should name search, questions, comparison, site-specific inventory and broker branding.

## Claims requiring approval

- exact languages and mixed-language behavior;
- installment-calculation support;
- name/icon/tone/welcome customization;
- chat visibility for company users;
- conversation retention and deletion;
- AI usage/credits model;
- WhatsApp handoff behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page is unmistakably about Client AI inside Estavo Sites.
- Brokers AI is mentioned only to clarify the difference or link back to Market.
- Data boundaries are visible in copy and art.
- The experience helps before asking for contact details.
- Every primary CTA goes to `/website/`.
- No high-intent, qualification or guaranteed-lead claim appears.
