# Landing Page Brief — Estavo Sites for Brokerage Companies

## Route and conversion

- Arabic route: `/enterprise/`
- English route: `/enterprise/en.html`
- Product: Estavo Sites
- Primary conversion: `https://estavo-brokers.com/website/`
- Primary event: `website_preview_started`
- Supporting enquiry event where needed: `integration_enquiry_submitted`

The page's main action starts the company website flow. A secondary custom-integration link may route to `/integrations/` when the company already has a platform it wants to keep.

## Why this page exists

A company website decision involves shared brand, governed inventory, team access, buyer tools and operating scope. That is materially different from a freelancer choosing a personal website, so it needs its own commercial page.

## Audience

- Brokerage owner or managing director.
- Sales director or team leader.
- Marketing/operations lead responsible for the company website.
- Company with fragmented project files or inconsistent sales presentation.

## Core proposition

Owner-approved positioning, 2026-09-20: large brokerages commonly carry the cost
of a website, technical support and people who enter and maintain property data.
The Estavo company proposition should sell the website, governed catalogue and
team foundation as costing less than the salary of one data-entry employee. This is a commercial
comparison, not a promise of guaranteed sales or a fixed public price; optional
scope is still confirmed during setup.

Arabic:

> موقع شركتك، وداتا السوق وفريقك على نفس الأساس.

English:

> Your brokerage website, market data and team on one shared foundation.

## Page sections

### 1. واجهة واحدة باسم الشركة

Company name, logo, colors, contact routes, Arabic/English and a consistent public property experience.

English: `One customer-facing property experience under the company name.`

### 2. داتا السوق ووحدات الشركة في كتالوج واحد

Show selected Estavo-maintained project data alongside company-entered primary/resale inventory, with ownership labels and update responsibility.

English: `Selected market data and company inventory in one governed catalogue.`

### 3. إدارة تناسب فريق، مش فرد واحد

Show supported roles and actions only after product validation: owner/admin, content/property management, lead/activity access and sales handoff.

Do not imply arbitrary enterprise permissions that do not exist.

English: `A site your team can operate together.`

### 4. Client AI يساعد العميل قبل المكالمة

Introduce buyer-facing AI inside the company site:

- uses company-approved inventory;
- asks, clarifies and compares;
- hands context to the team;
- does not replace sales.

Link: `/ai/`. Primary CTA remains `/website/`.

English: `Help buyers before the first sales call.`

### 5. سياق أوضح للمتابعة

Separate:

- contact details;
- preferences explicitly shared;
- pages/projects/units viewed;
- questions asked in Client AI.

Never label or score a person as qualified/high-intent from browsing alone.

English: `Give sales the context the buyer already created.`

### 6. Meta على حساب الشركة

Introduce the Meta connection as a Sites capability. The company owns the Meta account, audience and campaign decisions.

Link: `/meta/`.

English: `Connect permitted site events to the company's own Meta account.`

### 7. وسّع توزيع وحدات الشركة

Introduce eligible resale distribution to the Estavo Brokers network, company-controlled commission and no guarantee of sale.

Link: `/listings/`.

### 8. لو عندك منصة بالفعل

Give a clear fork:

- need a ready property website → continue with Estavo Sites;
- keep an existing site/platform and add Data + AI → `/integrations/`.

Do not make integration a third main product.

### 9. النطاق التجاري

Explain that company scope can depend on selected cities, team needs, AI usage, domain, data entry and custom integration. Avoid an instant-quote visual unless all values are approved.

### 10. FAQ

Recommended questions:

1. `إيه الفرق بين موقع الفرد وموقع الشركة؟`
2. `هل كل فريق الـ Sales يقدر يستخدمه؟`
3. `مين يدير الداتا والوحدات؟`
4. `هل Client AI يظهر باسم إستاڤو؟`
5. `نقدر نربط حساب Meta بتاع الشركة؟`
6. `لو عندنا CRM أو Website بالفعل نعمل إيه؟`
7. `إيه اللي بيحدد التكلفة؟`
8. `هل إستاڤو بيدير البيع أو الإعلانات بدلنا؟`

### 11. Closing conversion

- Arabic H2: `ابنِ تجربة واحدة باسم شركتك، لفريقك وعملاءك.`
- English H2: `Build one property experience for your brand, team and buyers.`
- CTA: `ابدأ موقع شركتك / Start your company website`
- Destination: `/website/`.

## Visual system

- More structured than the freelancer page, but not a generic enterprise dashboard.
- Primary silhouette: public company website plus one attached admin/team rail.
- Use navy for the governed core and paper/white for public customer surfaces.
- Avoid six equal feature icons; show one operating relationship.

## SEO

- Arabic title direction: `موقع عقاري لشركة الوساطة — Estavo Sites`
- English title direction: `Property website for brokerage companies — Estavo Sites`
- Description should mention company brand, shared inventory, buyer tools and team operation.

## Claims requiring approval

- roles and permission levels;
- number of team users;
- Client AI and activity access;
- Meta feature availability;
- company pricing/domain terms;
- 5,000-broker network count;
- Management AI, if mentioned at all.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page is explicitly about a brokerage company's Estavo Site.
- The main CTA starts `/website/`, not a generic enquiry.
- Existing-platform visitors have a clear secondary integration route.
- Team and buyer claims match live permissions.
- The company remains owner of brand, Meta account, inventory decisions and sales relationship.
