# Landing Page Brief — Sell Properties Through the Broker Network

## Route and conversion

- Arabic route: `/listings/`
- English route: `/listings/en.html`
- Product: Estavo Sites
- Capability: own-property publishing and broker-network distribution
- Primary conversion: `https://estavo-brokers.com/website/`
- Primary event: `website_preview_started`
- Supporting event after flow begins: `listing_flow_started`

Every primary CTA starts an Estavo Site. Do not route the main action to Estavo Market, WhatsApp or a generic enquiry.

## Why this page exists

This page explains one connected workflow: the broker adds a property to their own branded Site and can distribute eligible resale inventory to the Estavo Brokers network with a commission they control.

The page is not a general listing portal pitch. The property remains attached to the original broker/company, and Estavo is the presentation/distribution layer rather than the party taking over the deal.

## Audience

- Freelance broker with resale inventory.
- Brokerage company managing its own primary/resale units.
- Property owner represented by a broker, where the service model permits it.
- Site customer seeking additional broker reach.

## Core proposition

Validated-number version:

> وحدتك على موقعك. وقدام 7,000 بروكر يساعدوك تبيعها.
> Your property on your website—and in front of 7,000 brokers who can help sell it.

Fallback until the count is approved:

> وحدتك على موقعك. وقدام شبكة بروكرز أوسع.  
> Your property on your website—and across a wider broker network.

## Page sections

### 1. ضيف العقار مرة واحدة

Show the property fields needed for a useful listing: type, area, location, status, images, price where appropriate and contact information.

English: `Add the property once, with the details brokers and buyers need.`

Use an example label and do not invent a public price.

### 2. اعرضه على موقعك باسمك

The property appears beside the projects and units the broker chooses to present on their Estavo Site.

English: `Present it first under your own name.`

CTA: `ابدأ موقعك / Start your site` → `/website/`.

### 3. وصّله لشبكة Estavo Brokers

Explain the eligible resale distribution step. Describe network reach without publishing the 5,000 count until it is validated and dated.

English: `Make eligible resale inventory available to the Estavo Brokers network.`

### 4. إنت اللي بتحدد العمولة

Show the commission as a field explicitly controlled by the listing broker/company and visible in the network context.

English: `You decide the commission brokers see.`

Do not promise that Estavo takes no fee unless the exact commercial model is approved.

### 5. الـ Deal يفضل معاك

State the roles clearly:

- Estavo provides the publishing/distribution layer;
- other brokers can discover the opportunity;
- the original broker/company remains responsible for contact, verification, negotiation and sale.

English: `The relationship and deal remain with you.`

### 6. حدّث الحالة لما الوحدة تتغير

The customer is responsible for keeping self-entered inventory current unless a managed service explicitly covers it. Show edit, unpublish/sold and correction states.

English: `Keep the listing current when its status changes.`

### 7. مش عايز تدخل الوحدات بنفسك؟

Introduce optional managed data entry. Publish unit counts and price only after operations/finance approval.

English: `Need help entering the inventory?`

### 8. من موقعك للشبكة—في رحلة واحدة

Use a simple process strip:

1. create the Site;
2. add property;
3. review/publish;
4. enable eligible network distribution;
5. set commission;
6. update status.

CTA: `اعمل موقعك واعرض وحداتك / Create your site and list your properties` → `/website/`.

- 7,000-broker count, confirmed 22 September 2026;
- eligible listing/property/account types;
- commission display and fee model;

## Acceptance criteria

- The page begins with the customer's Site, not a generic marketplace.
- The original broker/company remains owner of the listing relationship and commission decision.
- Network reach is not described as guaranteed sale.
- The 5,000 claim appears only after validation; the fallback works without it.
- Every primary CTA goes to `/website/`.
- Primary/resale scope and update responsibility are explicit.
