Estavo Sites CRM Integration overview

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 3, 2026

This is the privacy policy for Estavo Sites CRM ("Estavo," "we," or "us"). It explains the personal information the CRM collects and how it accesses, uses, stores, shares, retains, and deletes Google user data when a website administrator optionally connects Google Calendar.

1. Who operates the service

Estavo operates Estavo Sites CRM from Cairo, Egypt. Questions, privacy requests, and complaints can be sent to support@estavo.space.

2. Information Estavo Sites CRM collects

  • Administrator and account information: name, business contact details, authentication records, assigned role, and the website or brokerage the administrator manages.
  • Lead and CRM information: names, contact details, form answers, property preferences, messages, notes, assigned agent, pipeline stage, tasks, appointments, showings, and related follow-up history.
  • Website and advertising information: website enquiries, consent records, source and campaign attribution, and interaction events used to operate reporting and integrations selected by the customer.
  • Technical information: security logs, IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, diagnostics, and feature usage needed to protect and improve the service.

3. Why we use personal information

We use this information to provide and secure the real-estate website and CRM, receive and route enquiries, maintain buyer profiles, schedule and record follow-up, deliver customer-selected integrations, provide support, prevent abuse, comply with law, and improve service reliability. We process it to perform our agreement with customers, follow user instructions, support legitimate service and security interests, meet legal duties, and handle consent where the law requires it.

4. Google data we access

After an authorized website administrator grants permission, Estavo accesses:

  • the Google account identifier and email address, so the CRM can show which account is connected;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, so the connection can continue without requiring a new sign-in for every update; and
  • permission to create one dedicated Estavo CRM calendar and to create, update, and delete events only in calendars created by this application.

The Google Calendar permission does not allow Estavo to read, update, or delete the user's other calendars. Estavo uses deterministic event identifiers to update only events created for Estavo CRM activities.

5. How Google data is used

Google data is used only to provide the user-facing calendar synchronization feature. A pending CRM task, appointment, or showing can create a Google Calendar event containing its title, scheduled start and end, reminder, location, notes, and the related lead's name and available contact details. When that CRM activity changes, Estavo updates the same event. When it is completed or cancelled, Estavo removes that event while the connection remains active.

6. Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest in Estavo's production database.
  • The connected Google account identifier, email, synchronization timestamps, and limited diagnostic error text are stored with the website's isolated CRM connection record.
  • Access is restricted to the website and authorized administrators under Estavo's tenant access controls.
  • Data is transmitted to Google using encrypted HTTPS connections.

7. Sharing and prohibited uses

We do not sell personal information or Google user data. We may use infrastructure, communications, analytics, security, and support processors that help us operate Estavo under our instructions and confidentiality obligations. Customer-directed integrations receive only the information needed to perform the function the administrator enables. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, credit decisions, surveillance, or training generalized artificial-intelligence models.

Estavo's access, use, storage, and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Retention, disconnection, and deletion

CRM information is retained while the customer account is active and afterward only as needed for customer instructions, legitimate records, dispute resolution, security, backup rotation, and legal duties. Retention periods depend on the type of record and applicable law.

Google connection data and encrypted tokens are retained only while the website's calendar connection remains active. An authorized website administrator can select Disconnect in the CRM. Estavo then requests revocation of the Google authorization and deletes its stored connection record and tokens. The dedicated Estavo calendar and events already present in Google Calendar remain under the user's control and can be deleted there.

Users can also revoke access from their Google Account connections. To request deletion of associated Estavo account data, email support@estavo.space. We process verified deletion requests subject to legal retention duties and backup rotation.

9. User choice and legal basis

The integration is optional. We process Google user data to perform the calendar-sync service the user explicitly requests. The connected administrator can withdraw that authorization at any time by disconnecting the integration.

10. Privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, a person may ask to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete personal information associated with them. Send a request to support@estavo.space. We may verify identity and authority before acting on a request. A person may also complain to their local data-protection authority.

11. Children and international processing

Estavo Sites CRM is a business service and is not directed to children. Service providers may process information in countries other than the user's own; where required, we use contractual and organizational safeguards for those transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page before materially changing how the integration uses Google user data. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
