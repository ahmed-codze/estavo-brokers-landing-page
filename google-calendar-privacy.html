Estavo Sites CRM Integration overview

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 3, 2026

This policy explains how Estavo Sites CRM ("Estavo," "we," or "us") handles Google user data when a website administrator optionally connects Google Calendar. It supplements Estavo's general product privacy disclosures.

1. Who operates the service

Estavo operates Estavo Sites CRM from Cairo, Egypt. Questions, privacy requests, and complaints can be sent to support@estavo.space.

2. Google data we access

After an authorized website administrator grants permission, Estavo accesses:

  • the Google account identifier and email address, so the CRM can show which account is connected;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, so the connection can continue without requiring a new sign-in for every update; and
  • Google Calendar event access needed to create, update, and delete Estavo-generated events in the connected account's primary calendar.

Estavo does not read or import the user's unrelated Google Calendar events. It uses deterministic event identifiers to update only events created for Estavo CRM activities.

3. How Google data is used

Google data is used only to provide the user-facing calendar synchronization feature. A pending CRM task, appointment, or showing can create a Google Calendar event containing its title, scheduled start and end, reminder, location, notes, and the related lead's name and available contact details. When that CRM activity changes, Estavo updates the same event. When it is completed or cancelled, Estavo removes that event while the connection remains active.

4. Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest in Estavo's production database.
  • The connected Google account identifier, email, synchronization timestamps, and limited diagnostic error text are stored with the website's isolated CRM connection record.
  • Access is restricted to the website and authorized administrators under Estavo's tenant access controls.
  • Data is transmitted to Google using encrypted HTTPS connections.

5. Sharing and prohibited uses

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use it for advertising, credit decisions, surveillance, or training generalized artificial-intelligence models. We do not transfer it to third parties except infrastructure processors that help us securely operate the service, where they act under our instructions and confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Estavo's access, use, storage, and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention, disconnection, and deletion

The Google connection data and encrypted tokens are retained only while the website's calendar connection remains active. An authorized website administrator can select Disconnect in the CRM. Estavo then requests revocation of the Google authorization and deletes its stored connection record and tokens. Events already present in Google Calendar remain under the user's control and can be deleted there.

Users can also revoke access from their Google Account connections. To request deletion of associated Estavo account data, email support@estavo.space. We process verified deletion requests subject to legal retention duties and backup rotation.

7. User choice and legal basis

The integration is optional. We process Google user data to perform the calendar-sync service the user explicitly requests. The connected administrator can withdraw that authorization at any time by disconnecting the integration.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page before materially changing how the integration uses Google user data. The effective date above shows the latest revision.
