Where to find and verify developer inventory in Egypt

By Estavo Brokers · Published 2 October 2026 · Practical guide

Find developer inventory through authorized developer contacts, official project materials and a maintained broker platform. For every offer, verify the unit, source, payment terms and update date; confirm availability again before a booking commitment.

Choose a source for each job

An official brochure helps explain a project. A dated price list helps establish quoted terms. A responsible sales or reservations contact confirms booking status. An inventory platform helps you search and compare. These sources solve different jobs: a brochure is not confirmation that a specific unit is available today.

Keep an inventory verification record

Fields to check for each shortlisted offer
FieldWhat to record
IdentityDeveloper, project, phase, building, unit number or model
SpecificationArea definition, type, floor, view and finishing
PriceCurrency, quoted total, cash/instalment basis and validity
PaymentsBooking, down payment, dated instalments and extra charges
StatusAvailable, held, sold or not confirmed; source and check time
DeliveryPublished or contract date, phase and verification status

Do not confuse an upload date with an offer date

A PDF filename, website upload timestamp or spreadsheet creation date can describe the document rather than the economic terms. Preserve the date label exactly and ask whether the quote is still valid. If no effective date is stated, record “effective date not confirmed.” Never turn an old document into a current offer because you downloaded it today.

Resolve conflicting availability before promising it

If a platform shows available stock and the developer says it is sold, pause that option and request confirmation. Check whether both sources refer to the same phase, building and unit. Report the discrepancy for correction rather than continuing to use the older status.

A sold marker is also not proof of the achieved sale price. An old inventory row may retain a list price after its status changes.

Ask for the missing terms explicitly

“Please confirm the status of [project / phase / building / unit], the current quoted price and currency, valid payment plan, booking amount, additional charges and delivery basis. What is the effective date of these terms?”

Use the reply to update your shortlist and keep the confirmation with the offer record. If a field remains unknown, mark it unknown in the proposal. Do not substitute a similar unit’s terms without saying so.

Questions brokers ask

Is a downloaded price list current inventory?

Not necessarily. Check the effective date, exact unit and current status with the responsible source.

Can I treat a sold row as a transaction price?

No. A sold label with a stored asking price does not establish the achieved price or sale date.
