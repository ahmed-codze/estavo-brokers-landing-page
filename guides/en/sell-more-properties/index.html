How to sell more properties as a broker in Egypt

By Estavo Brokers · Published 2 October 2026 · Practical guide

To improve property sales, make each step of the buyer journey clearer: qualify the budget and timing, verify suitable inventory, explain a short comparison and agree one next action. Track where opportunities stall before spending more on leads.

Find the step that is losing opportunities

Review your last 20 enquiries using a simple sheet: enquiry source, first response, qualified need, shortlist sent, viewing booked, offer made and outcome. Count how many enquiries reach each step. These are your own operating measures, not industry benchmarks.

If enquiries rarely become qualified conversations, improve your opening questions. If buyers receive a shortlist but do not view, check location, affordability and delivery requirements. If viewings do not progress, record the actual objection instead of assuming price is always the problem.

Qualify the buyer before searching

  • Purpose: home, second home or investment?
  • Preferred area and acceptable alternatives?
  • Total price ceiling, cash available now and comfortable payment schedule?
  • Unit type, bedrooms, minimum area and finishing?
  • Required handover date and readiness to book?
  • Who else needs to approve the decision?

Repeat the brief back to the client. Distinguish a hard limit from a preference: a delivery deadline may rule out a project even when its advertised price looks attractive.

Build a shortlist the client can decide from

Present a small set of defensible options rather than forwarding every file you have. For each option, state why it fits, its main compromise and the item you still need to verify. Compare equivalent unit types and payment structures. A lower advertised total does not necessarily mean a lighter upfront commitment.

Estavo Market can help you search project and unit information, ask the broker AI about the request and compare available details. Treat fast-changing prices, availability and booking terms as provisional until the responsible source confirms them.

Send an offer that answers the next questions

Use one readable proposal containing the project, exact unit or model, location, area, finishing, delivery information, payment terms, source/check date and your contact details. Mark unknown items openly. Estavo’s branded PDF Offer brings the available information into a document you can send to the buyer; review it before sending.

Finish with a choice the client can make: “Which of these two locations suits your daily commute better?” or “Shall we verify this unit and arrange a viewing?” A proposal without a next step can leave both sides waiting.

Follow up with a reason, not another generic reminder

Agree the next contact time and channel. Follow up with a useful answer: clarification of a payment milestone, a confirmed availability update or a comparison against the buyer’s stated concern. Stop or reduce contact when the client asks you to.

“You said delivery timing was the deciding factor. I have checked the information for the two shortlisted options. Would you like us to review that difference before choosing a viewing?”

This is a reusable message template, not evidence of a completed customer transaction.

Review the funnel every week

Measure qualified-enquiry-to-viewing and viewing-to-offer conversion on consistent definitions. Record lost reasons such as unaffordable upfront cash, wrong delivery date, no suitable stock or no response. Compare periods with similar lead sources and sample sizes. Change one weak step and observe your own results; do not attribute every change to a tool.

Questions brokers ask

Does Estavo guarantee more sales?

No. Estavo supports research, comparisons and offer preparation. Sales depend on inventory, buyer fit, follow-up and market conditions.

Should I buy more leads first?

First identify where existing enquiries stop progressing. More leads will not automatically solve weak qualification or unsuitable shortlists.
