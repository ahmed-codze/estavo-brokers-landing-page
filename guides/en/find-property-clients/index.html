How to find property clients as a broker in Egypt

By Estavo Brokers · Published 2 October 2026 · Practical guide

Find property clients by choosing a clear buyer segment, answering its real questions and giving people a simple way to contact you. Build genuine referrals, track qualified enquiries by source and follow up with consent and useful information.

Choose a specific client problem

Define the people you can serve well: home buyers in a chosen area, buyers needing a particular handover window or owners seeking a suitable resale route. Avoid claiming expertise in every area at once. Your positioning should describe the client’s problem and the evidence you can provide.

For example, “I help families compare New Cairo homes against their payment and delivery needs” is more actionable than “all properties, best prices.” This is an illustrative positioning statement, not a verified business claim.

Publish answers that show how you work

Build a small library around recurring questions: upfront cash, cash versus instalment quotes, finishing, delivery and comparing two projects. Use your own explanations and permitted visuals. Give sources and check dates when discussing offers. Avoid presenting old prices as current stock.

End each useful post with a relevant action: send a brief, request a comparison or book a call. Publish enough detail that the answer helps even if the reader never contacts you.

Build referral relationships with a clear handoff

Ask satisfied clients and professional contacts for genuine introductions where appropriate. Explain which enquiries you can handle and what information helps: purpose, area, budget and timeline. Agree responsibilities with cooperating brokers and respect confidentiality.

Never publish a client’s name, budget, phone number or transaction story without permission. Use anonymous operating notes for internal learning, not invented public testimonials.

Give prospects one clear contact path

A mobile-friendly page should identify you, your coverage and the next step. Keep a contact form brief: name, preferred contact method and an initial description of the need. Follow up to qualify the rest. Explain how the information will be used and respect a request to stop contact.

Test channels against qualified enquiries

For paid channels, cost per qualified enquiry equals channel spend divided by qualified enquiries in that period. If there are none, do not report a reassuring average. Small samples should guide a test, not support broad performance claims.

A four-week plan you can execute

  1. Week one: choose your segment, prepare a brief form and record a baseline.
  2. Week two: publish answers to three recurring buyer questions and share them through your legitimate channels.
  3. Week three: ask for genuine introductions and follow up on agreed conversations with useful comparisons.
  4. Week four: review qualified enquiries and progression; keep the activity that fits your segment and improve the weak step.

Questions brokers ask

No guarantee of leads is made here. Market supports broker research and offers; Estavo Sites is a separate website service.

What should I measure first?

Record the source, qualified need and next action for each enquiry. Raw lead counts alone do not show whether a channel brings suitable buyers.
