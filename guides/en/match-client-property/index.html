How to match a client with the right property

By Estavo Brokers · Published 2 October 2026 · Practical guide

Match properties by checking the buyer’s hard constraints first, then ranking preferences. Compare affordability, location, specification and delivery on the same basis, and explain the trade-off behind each shortlisted option.

Turn the conversation into a usable brief

Separate four groups: purpose, financial capacity, physical requirements and timing. Total budget alone is incomplete: a buyer may afford the headline price but not the booking or later milestone payments. Ask how much cash is available now and when additional funds become available.

Write one sentence the buyer can confirm: “You need [type] in [area], with [minimum requirements], within [total and upfront limits], for delivery by [date].”

Filter hard constraints before ranking preferences

Hard constraints are requirements that rule out an option: maximum upfront cash, required handover date or accessibility needs. Preferences help rank remaining options: a particular view, floor or amenity. Ask the client which category each requirement belongs to. Never silently relax a hard constraint to produce more results.

  • Reject options that exceed a confirmed financial limit.
  • Flag missing delivery or finishing information.
  • Keep acceptable alternative locations separate from the requested area.
  • If nothing fits, explain which constraint prevents a match.

Use this research prompt

“Help me shortlist properties for this buyer: [purpose], [city/area], [type and rooms], [minimum area], [total budget], [cash available now], [payment capacity], [delivery deadline], [finishing] and [non-negotiables]. Use information available in Estavo Market. For each option explain the fit, compromise and missing information. Do not invent availability or relax the limits without asking.”

This is a reusable prompt, not a claim that every request will return a match. Review the AI answer against the underlying project and unit information.

Explain the compromise behind the recommendation

Say “This fits your upfront limit but needs confirmation of the delivery date,” rather than “This is the best investment.” A property can be suitable for a home buyer and unsuitable for another client’s rental or resale plan. Keep your recommendation tied to the confirmed brief.

If no option fits, discuss one explicit change at a time: alternative area, smaller unit, later handover or different payment capacity. Obtain the buyer’s agreement before searching the revised brief.

Create the next action from the shortlist

Ask the buyer which trade-off they prefer, confirm the exact unit and current terms, then arrange a viewing or prepare a reviewed PDF Offer. Estavo Market supports the research, comparison and offer preparation; the broker and buyer remain responsible for the final choice.

Record why each option was included and why the others were rejected. That short record makes your next conversation more useful than starting the search again.

Questions brokers ask

What if no property fits the request?

Explain which hard constraint prevents a match, then ask whether the buyer wants to change one requirement.

Can AI make the final property decision?

AI can organize available information and suggest options. The broker and buyer must assess suitability and confirm material details.
