Google Calendar integration

Keep every lead follow-up on your calendar.

Estavo Sites CRM is the customer workspace included with Estavo-powered real-estate websites. It collects website enquiries, organizes buyer follow-up, and can add CRM tasks, appointments, and showings to the Google Calendar you connect.

Learn about Estavo How we use Google data

What the connection does

Adds follow-ups

A CRM task, appointment, or showing is added to the connected account's primary Google Calendar.

Keeps changes current

If the scheduled time or CRM details change, Estavo updates the same calendar event. Completed or cancelled CRM activities are removed.

Works per website

Each customer website has its own connection. A website administrator controls which Google account is connected and can disconnect it at any time.

Why Estavo requests Google access

Estavo requests your Google account email to identify the connected account and Google Calendar event access solely to create, update, and delete the CRM follow-up events described above.

  • Calendar sync is optional and starts only after a website administrator chooses Sync Google and grants access.
  • Estavo does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and kept only while the website remains connected.
  • Disconnecting in the CRM revokes Google's authorization and deletes the stored connection tokens.
Full details are available in the Estavo Sites CRM privacy policy. For help or a data-deletion request, email support@estavo.space.
